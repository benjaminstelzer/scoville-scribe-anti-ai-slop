# Fidelity Modes

Use this reference when the requested operation changes the relation between a
source and its output. Apply only the relevant sections to each segment.

## Contents

- Adapt and summarize
- Localize
- Source-exact
- Regulated content
- Author-owned content
- Variant-controlled content
- Verify fidelity

## Adapt and summarize

Remove only information the requested form can omit while preserving the
conditions that keep every surviving claim true. Do not convert a comparison
into an absolute claim, a possibility into a guarantee, a bounded result into a
general effect, or a current state into a promised future state.

When the source enumerates completion conditions, status criteria, risks, or
exceptions, account for each before compressing. An aggregate status or passing
test cannot stand in for an unverified condition. Preserve numbers, units,
denominators, comparison arms, time windows, and sample limits that change how a
result should be interpreted.

Adapt voice, order, and detail to the target surface only after the claim set is
fixed. When a hard length limit forces a material omission, identify the omitted
claim rather than quietly weakening it.

## Localize

Preserve meaning, canonical product terminology, register, modality, negation,
and the relationship between problem, action, and consequence. Natural target-
language grammar is allowed; literal source-language syntax is not a fidelity
requirement unless the request makes it one.

Keep runtime structure byte-safe where required: placeholders, interpolation
tokens, format specifiers, escape sequences, markup, keyboard tokens, command
names, identifiers, and pluralization keys. Do not translate code, API names, or
product terms without an owned localized form. Preserve destructive-warning
strength and accessibility purpose.

If the target locale has no verified canonical term, use the least surprising
accurate wording and flag the terminology decision when it affects reuse.

## Source-exact

Extract or quote without stylistic change. Preserve spelling, punctuation,
capitalization, whitespace, line breaks, and tokens within the exact boundary.
Do not silently correct a source error. If correction was not requested, keep
the exact text and report the suspected defect outside it.

State the exact boundary before extraction when markers, fields, or included
lines are ambiguous. Source-exact mode does not authorize quoting more material
than the user or applicable rights permit.

## Regulated content

Use authoritative sources for legal, medical, financial, safety, privacy, and
other regulated claims. Preserve qualifications, jurisdiction, effective date,
duty holder, exceptions, and review status. Do not turn editorial cleanup into
substantive advice, certification, or a compliance claim.

When qualified review is still required, label the draft accordingly and bind
the limitation to the affected claim. Polished language is not evidence of
approval.

## Author-owned content

Rewrite only to the degree requested. Preserve attribution, speaking position,
personal experience, relationships, uncertainty, and the stated basis and
strength of retained opinions or decisions. Do not manufacture feelings,
confidence, biography, or first-person experience to make the text vivid.

When style samples are supplied, learn their observable register and structure;
do not copy unrelated claims or signature phrases.

## Variant-controlled content

Change only the variable the comparison intends to test. Hold facts, length,
structure, examples, calls to action, and formatting constant unless the test
defines them as variables. Record any unavoidable coupled change before
interpreting preference or performance.

Do not let one variant receive extra factual checking, stronger evidence, or
more favorable formatting. A comparison between several changed dimensions
cannot identify which dimension caused the result.

## Verify fidelity

Compare the final segment with the authoritative source for the dimensions its
mode protects. For summaries, map every surviving claim back to its condition
and evidence. For localization, compare tokens mechanically and meaning
semantically. For source-exact text, use a byte or exact-string comparison when
the boundary permits it. Report any intentional deviation explicitly.
